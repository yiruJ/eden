# Eden Music Academy: Fee Policy

Two deliverables below.

1. **Public guarantee** for the website, beneath the rates on the pricing page. Says nothing about legacy pricing.
2. **Email to legacy families only** (enrolled before 1 August 2026). Contains the specifics.

Plus the internal notes on how to run it.

**Design principle:** nothing public reveals that a cheaper tier exists. Families paying current rates never learn that anyone pays less, because that information can only make them feel worse and helps nobody.

---

## 1. Public guarantee (website, under the pricing table)

Written as reassurance for people about to buy, not as disclosure. It makes committing to a rate easier, and mentions no dates, tiers, or legacy pricing.

### Our fee promise

**Your package is locked in.** Once you have paid for a 5 or 10 week package, it is honoured in full at the rate you paid, whatever happens to our rates in the meantime.

**You will always get notice.** If our rates change, families already enrolled keep their existing rate until the change date, and we give at least two months' written notice beforehand.

Any questions, call us on 0410 385 227 or email info@edenmusicacademy.com.

## 3. Internal notes

### What this email deliberately does not do

- **It does not state the March increase amount.** It commits to a date and a process only. If the new-price cohort data in November suggests softening to a two-step increase, that remains available without breaking a promise.
- **It does not apologise.** A first rate rise after growing to five teachers and a new studio needs no apology. Apologising signals that the increase is unreasonable.
- **It does not go to families on current rates.** They would learn that others pay less, which can only reduce their satisfaction with a price they have already accepted. Nothing is gained by telling them.
- **It does not name the old or new rate.** Legacy families already know what they pay. Printing both figures side by side invites comparison arithmetic and makes the March change feel larger than experiencing it will.
- **It does not promise an annual review.** Publishing a review cycle would tell families to expect a rise every year, create an anxiety that does not currently exist, and box you in if you need to move off-cycle. The two public commitments give you the reusable template without the schedule.

### Teacher script, if a parent asks

To a **legacy** family:
> "Our rates went up for new enrolments recently. Yours is held until March, and we emailed you the details."

To a **current-rate** family:
> "That's our standard rate. Whatever you've paid for is locked in, and we'd give you two months' notice before anything changed."

Every teacher should give the same answer. Consistency matters more than detail, and no teacher should volunteer that legacy families pay less.

### Timeline

| When | Action | Audience |
|---|---|---|
| Now | Publish new rates and the fee promise on website. | Public |
| Now | Send the email. Brief teachers on the script. | Legacy families (38) |
| Nov / Dec 2026 | Review renewal rate of the new-price cohort. Confirm or adjust the March plan. | Internal |
| Week of 28 Jan 2027 | Operational reminder with the specific new rate. | Legacy families only |
| 1 Mar 2027 | New rates apply to packages purchased on or after this date. | Legacy families |

No fixed review cycle is published or implied. When a future increase is needed, the same three commitments apply: package honoured, existing rate held to the change date, two months' notice. That is the reusable template, and it works without telling families to expect a rise every year.

### What to track before the January decision

Record every non-renewal in the new-price cohort with a stated reason. The question to answer in November is: **is the post-August cohort renewing at a rate near the current 87%?**

- **At or near 87%:** proceed with the single-step increase to full rates on 1 March.
- **Materially below:** move to a two-step transition, roughly $45 in March and $50 in September 2027, and consider whether the new-student price itself needs revisiting.

This is why the email commits to a date rather than a number.

### System changes needed before launch

- Add `Price tier` (Legacy / Current) to the Private Lesson Packages database.
- Add `Discount type` and `Discount %` fields, which do not currently exist anywhere. Discounts are presently applied from memory and leave no record, so the 21 of 61 packages that are below list price cannot be audited or explained.
- Both are needed from day one so the legacy cohort is identifiable without inference.
